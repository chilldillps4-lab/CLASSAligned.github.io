From Research to Reality: My Journey with SGX3 and

Reflecting on my time at SGX3, "amazing" feels like an understatement. It was a period of intense
growth, high-level collaboration, and the kind of hands on research that truly changes your
perspective on technology.One of my proudest contributions was helping bridge the gap between abstract concepts and a
tangible identity. I was part of the team that developed the name CLASSAligned for our AI helper. It
wasn’t just about a catchy title; it was about finding a name that reflected our mission to create
ethical, supportive, and accessible AI technology.Transitioning into the technical side of the project, I spent significant time working within the group’s
GitHub repository. This is where the real "flourishing" happened for me. Having the guidance of our
leads, Joshua and Qimora, was invaluable. They didn’t just manage the project; they mentored us,
ensuring we understood the why behind the code. I also owe a debt of gratitude to Jamie and Dr. Hayden. Their support throughout the entire research
process provided the framework I needed to push my boundaries and contribute meaningfully to the
team’s goals.As we reached the final stages of our tasks, the synergy within my immediate group became our
greatest asset. Working alongside Suniyah and Abraham was a masterclass in teamwork. Their
perseverance and "never-quit" attitude kept us ahead of our deadlines. They weren't just partners;
they were the engine that kept our project moving forward through every technical hurdle. While I wasn't able to attend the ADMI (Association of Computer Science Departments at Minority
Institutions) conference in person this year, the energy the team brought back was infectious.
Hearing their stories and the positive reception of our work made me feel like I was right there with
them. It served as a powerful reminder of the impact our research has on the wider academic and
tech community.This experience was a once in a lifetime opportunity that I wouldn’t trade for anything. To anyone
reading this: I highly encourage you to look into the work we’ve done. We poured our hard work and
passion into this project, and I am incredibly proud of where we landed.
